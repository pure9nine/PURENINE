Ridddle App Privacy Policy

July 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ridddle (“the app,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use it. Ridddle is developed and operated by PURENINE Studios Inc (“the developer”).

We’ve designed Ridddle to be privacy-friendly. In short: the app tells jokes and reveals answers, and that’s it. We don’t ask you to create an account, and we don’t collect personal information about you.

We don’t collect personal information directly. However, as described below, third-party services we use (like Google AdMob) may collect certain device identifiers for advertising purposes.

Ridddle does not require you to register, log in, or provide any personal details such as your name, email address, or phone number. We have no servers that store information about you, and we do not build a profile of you or your activity.

Any settings or preferences you choose within the app (for example, theme or display options) are stored locally on your device only. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. If you delete the app, this information is removed with it.

To keep the app running well, we use the following third-party services, which may collect limited information automatically (such as a device identifier, general usage data, or diagnostic information) in accordance with their own privacy policies:

Google AdMob - Advertising. Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We do not control how these third parties handle data. We encourage you to review their privacy policies. We do not combine this information with any personal data, because we do not collect personal data ourselves.

Ridddle does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). The app is suitable for general audiences and does not require any personal information to use.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with anyone, because we do not collect it.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. Because we do not collect or store any personal data, we generally have no such information to provide, correct, or delete. If you have questions about your rights, you can contact us using the details below.

Because the app does not collect or transmit personal information, there is no personal data for us to secure on our servers. Any preferences stored on your device are protected by your device’s own security features.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the “Effective date” at the top of this page and, where appropriate, provide notice within the app. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Ridddle displays ads served by Google AdMob. To provide relevant ads, AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers (such as the IDFA), general device and app usage data, and approximate location. On iOS, you’ll be asked for permission via Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework before any tracking-related data is collected; you can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. For more on how Google handles this data, see Google’s Partner Sites & Apps policy.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at:

hello@purenine.com
